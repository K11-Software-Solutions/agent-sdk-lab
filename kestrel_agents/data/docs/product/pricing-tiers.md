## Free
The Free plan is for up to 5 users and includes boards, sprints and basic reports.

## Team
The Team plan costs $12 per user per month, billed annually. It adds unlimited users, advanced reports and integrations.

## Enterprise
The Enterprise plan has custom pricing; customers contact sales for a quote. It adds single sign-on, audit logs and a dedicated support contact.
