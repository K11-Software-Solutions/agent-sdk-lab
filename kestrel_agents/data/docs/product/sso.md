## Availability
Single sign-on is available on the Enterprise plan only.

## Protocols and providers
Kestrel supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. Okta and Microsoft Entra ID are tested on every release; other identity providers usually work but are not tested.

## Enforcement
Workspace admins can require SSO for all members. When SSO is enforced, password sign-in is disabled.
