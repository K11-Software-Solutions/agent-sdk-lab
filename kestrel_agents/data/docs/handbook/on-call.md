## Rotation
Each product team runs a weekly on-call rotation. Handover happens every Monday at 10:00 local time, in the team channel, with a short written summary of open incidents.

## Paging and response times
Pages come through PagerDuty. For a SEV1 incident the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 15 minutes. For a SEV2 incident the response target is one hour. SEV3 issues are handled in working hours.

## Escalation
If the primary on-call engineer has not acknowledged a SEV1 page after 15 minutes, PagerDuty escalates to the secondary, then to the engineering manager.
